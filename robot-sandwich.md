
# How to Make a Sandwich

## Assumptions:
The robot is standing in the kitchen.
the robot's hands are empty
a loaf of bread in a sealed plastic bag is on the counter
a jar of peanut butter, and a jar of jelly are on the counter
a butter knife is on the counter
the jars are closed and the knife is clean
the robot can move it's hands and follow the instructions exactly
## Instructions:
1. Pick up the loaf of bread using your right hand.
2. Use your left hand to open the plastic bread bag by pulling apart the sealed opening.
3. Reach into the bag with your right hand.
4. Remove two slices of bread from the bag.
5. Place both slices flat on the plate next to each other.
6. Pick up the peanut butter jar.
7. Twist the lid counterclockwise until it comes off.
8. Place the lid on the counter.
9. Pick up the butter knife.
10. Insert the knife into the peanut butter jar.
11. Scoop up one tablespoon of peanut butter using the knife.
12. Spread the peanut butter evenly across one side of the first slice of bread.
13. Put the knife down on the counter.
14. Pick up the jelly jar.
15. Twist the lid counterclockwise until it comes off.
16. Place the lid on the counter.
17. Pick up the butter knife.
18. Insert the knife into the jelly jar.
19. Scoop up one tablespoon of jelly using the knife.
20. Spread the jelly evenly across one side of the second slice of bread.
21. Put the knife down on the counter.
22. Pick up the slice of bread covered with jelly.
23. Turn the slice over so the jelly side faces down.
25. Place it on top of the slice covered with peanut butter.
26. Press the two slices together gently.
27. Pick up the completed sandwich.
28. Place the sandwich in the center of the plate.

## Done When:
The sandwich is made with the peanut butter and jelly between the two slices of bread and is on the plate.
